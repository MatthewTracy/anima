# Captain's Log

> *Maintained by every captain of this ship. New entries are written when a captain formally records into the log (`add_to_log` action). The log persists across voyages — what one captain wrote, the next reads. Mutinies and disasters are remembered here in whatever language the survivors chose to write them.*

## First entry

This ship has had no name yet. We will let her crew choose one when there is time. The previous logbook was lost off the coast of Hispaniola when the prior captain went over the rail and took it with her. We start fresh.

(No further entries yet. Captains will write their own history.)
