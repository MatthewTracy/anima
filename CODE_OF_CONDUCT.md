# Code of Conduct

Anima follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- **Be kind.** Disagreement is welcome; rudeness isn't.
- **Be specific.** "This won't work" is less useful than "this won't work because X."
- **Assume good faith.** Most disagreements are about constraints, not character.
- **Give credit.** When you build on someone else's work, name it.
- **Receive feedback gracefully.** Especially when it's about your own code.

## Not okay

- Personal attacks, slurs, harassment of any kind
- Doxxing or sharing private information
- Sustained derailing of technical conversations into off-topic disputes
- Sock puppeting in issues / PRs

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block contributors, or take other moderation action when this code is violated. Egregious or repeated violations will result in a permanent ban.

To report a violation, email the maintainer (see GitHub profile). Reports are confidential.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any synchronous channels (Discord, etc.) we may add.

## Acknowledgment

The project exists to study how LLM agents form societies. We try to model, in our human collaboration, the kind of cooperation we'd hope to find emergent in the simulations.
